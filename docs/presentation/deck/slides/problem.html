The problem

Hitting a clay is a timing problem

20 m/s

Roughly how fast a crossing clay moves

~170 ms

From the decision to the shot arriving: see it, press the trigger, fly there

3.4 m

How far the clay travels in that time

A shooter solves this by feel. TBOT solves it by measuring every delay and firing early by exactly that much.

The shot pattern is well under a metre wide at these ranges, and the clay moves several metres between deciding and hitting. So you cannot shoot where the clay is; you shoot where it will be. People learn that lead over thousands of shots. My approach was to measure every delay in the chain and let the machine do the arithmetic. 170 ms is the measured total on the second range day; 3.4 m is just 20 m/s times that.
