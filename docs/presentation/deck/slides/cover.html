TBOT

A shotgun that decides when to pull its own trigger.

Hunter Edmonds

Open with the one-line idea: I swing the gun and hold a button, the machine picks the instant to fire. Everything bolted to the gun in this photo is 3D printed and clamps on; the gun itself is unmodified. This is the build as of this morning.
