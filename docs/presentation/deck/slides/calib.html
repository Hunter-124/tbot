Boresight checked at a second distance: within 0.067°.

Nov 16: focal length and boresight in a hallway

Each number the prediction depends on was measured with its own procedure. The boresight one is the nicest piece of metrology: a laser cartridge in the chamber is never perfectly centred, so I rotated it and took the centre of the circle it drew, which cancels the laser’s own error. A check at a second distance agreed to 0.067 degrees, against a shot pattern about 0.75 degrees in radius. Trigger delay is now measured from the recoil spike on every live shot.
