Safety

A person holds the button for every shot

1

Dry run by default. Without an explicit live setting the servo never moves.

2

Button interlock at the actuator. No button, no trigger press.

3

Release means rest. A cut wire or loose connector reads as not armed.

4

One shot per press.

5

Watchdog. No fresh image for 250 ms and the servo returns to rest.

Known gap

These are software layers. There is no hardware cut in the servo supply yet; unplugging the battery is what makes it safe.

Where it runs

Clay targets on a range, under range rules. All bench work with the gun unloaded.

The machine never fires on its own initiative. The arming button is a dead-man switch: normally open with a pull-up, so any wiring fault fails to the safe state. I am upfront about the gap: all five layers are software, and the double fire is exactly why a hardware cut in the servo supply belongs on the next-steps list.
