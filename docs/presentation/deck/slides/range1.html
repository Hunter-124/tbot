First live range day · Nov 17 · day 23

2 shots.

2 clays.

Engaged at 13 to 30 m. Then the printed servo mount broke.

The gun camera at the moment the shot arrived, 27.8 m. Circle: where the pattern went. Cross: where the clay was.

Twenty-three days after measuring the gun. Nine presses of the arming button, seven fire commands, two shots actually left the gun, and both broke their clay. Then the mount failed, which ended the day. The picture is the gun’s own camera at the instant the shot arrived: this one missed centre by 0.62 degrees against a 0.75 degree pattern, so a fringe hit. Honest footnote: the trigger delay in the settings was wrong by 60 ms that day, and both clays broke anyway.
