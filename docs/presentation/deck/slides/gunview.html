What the gun sees when it fires

Left: phone video.

Right: the gun’s own camera.

Yellow rings: objects it is tracking.

Red frame: the fire command. Recoil follows 66 ms later.

Shot at 24 m. The two videos are lined up on the bang: this shot and the one before it are 24.17 s apart on the phone and 24.177 s apart in the gun’s data.

Here the gun’s recording did survive. Phone on the left, the gun’s own view on the right with what it was tracking drawn on top. When the frame turns red, that is the command to the servo; the gun actually goes off 66 ms later, which is the delay from the timing slide showing up in real data. The two videos are synchronised by the sound of the shot and the recoil spike, and the pairing is confirmed by the spacing between two consecutive shots matching to 5 ms.
