Test ladder

Prove each step before adding ammunition

1 · Simulation

Synthetic clays

Simulated targets flown through a model of the calibrated camera, judged against ground truth.

2 · Bench

Screen in the loop

The gun’s camera watches a moving target on a monitor while the real servo fires against a microphone.

3 · Backyard

Real sky, dry gun

Tracking birds by hand with the gun unloaded, the morning of the first range day.

4 · Range

Live fire

Every press records images and motion data, so each shot can be replayed afterwards.

The first moving target: a hawk over the yard, followed by hand for 8 seconds

I did not want the first test of anything to be with a loaded gun. Simulation checked the logic. The bench rig timed the whole chain, photon to trigger break, with no ammunition. The backyard gave real sky and real swing. Only then the range. And every range press saves everything the gun saw, which is how I could later audit exactly what happened on each shot.
