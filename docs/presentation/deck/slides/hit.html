This is the last clip of the day and the clearest. Real time first, then four times slower with the clay enlarged in the corner. You can see it intact for about a tenth of a second after the muzzle blast, then it comes apart. The trigger is not in frame, but the red ready light on the front module is lit, meaning armed and live. Tally for the afternoon: the thrower counter reached 16 and at least 5 broke; on the others the gun did not fire, the computer was off, or we missed. One caveat to say out loud: the gun’s own recording of this particular shot was not kept. That afternoon the computer kept losing power at the shot, before it could save. That power problem is on the lessons slide.
