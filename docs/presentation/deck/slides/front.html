Front module

Camera, computer and battery in 231 g

Gun-left: Raspberry Pi 3B, camera in the nose

Gun-right: 3-cell battery, voltage regulators, harness

8 printed parts

Hung from two hose clamps seated in grooves

Motion sensor in the clamp

Rigid to the barrel, so it reads what the bore is doing

Lens locked against recoil

A printed M12 × 0.5 split clamp nut holds focus

Packaging was the main constraint: it has to sit under the barrel without blocking the sight picture or my support hand, survive recoil, and keep the camera rigidly aligned to the bore. The camera sits in the nose looking along the barrel. The Pi is on one side, the battery and regulators on the other, with the wiring harness and camera ribbon modelled in the assembly. Total mass 231 grams.
