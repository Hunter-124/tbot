This is the chain for a real shot on the second range day. The camera frame is about 55 ms old by the time a decision is made, the servo and trigger take about 66 ms to release the hammer and ignite, and the shot takes about 47 ms to reach a clay at 14 metres. Bars are to scale. The grey bar is the first trigger mechanism: 161 ms on its own. A known delay can be predicted across; an inconsistent one cannot, so the measured 4.9 ms jitter in the trigger is the number that really limits accuracy.
