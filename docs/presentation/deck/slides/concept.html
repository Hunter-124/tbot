How it works

The shooter swings, the machine picks the instant

1 · See

Camera

A small camera under the barrel looks along the bore and finds the clay dozens of times a second.

2 · Sense

Motion sensor

A gyro and accelerometer in the clamp measure how the gun itself is swinging.

3 · Predict

Lead

Where will the clay be, and where will the barrel point, once every delay has passed?

4 · Press

Servo

When the two line up, a hobby servo presses the trigger. One shot per button press.

The trigger can only move while the shooter holds an arming button.

Keep this at the block-diagram level. Camera finds the target, the motion sensor separates the clay’s motion from my swing, the computer extrapolates both forward by the total delay, and the servo presses when the predicted shot covers the predicted clay. I still aim and swing; the machine only chooses when. The software was written with heavy AI assistance, so this talk is about the hardware and the measurements that make it work.
