Reverse engineering

Scanning the gun with a phone

The forend and trigger guard are compound curves that calipers cannot capture, so the clamps were modelled against a scan.

Scan (white) over the CAD body (red), in millimetres

The gun has almost no flat or cylindrical reference surfaces where the parts needed to grip. Rather than iterate test prints against guesses, I scanned it. Printed boards with known square size give absolute scale, good to about two thirds of a millimetre. The mesh was then aligned to the same coordinate frame as the CAD, and the clamp surfaces were modelled directly against it. First full CAD was Oct 26; the scan and rebuild were Nov 5.
