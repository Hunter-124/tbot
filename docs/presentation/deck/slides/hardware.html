The hardware

Two printed assemblies, nothing on the gun modified

Trigger set: servo, cam, arming button

Front module: camera, computer, battery, motion sensor

12 printed parts

PLA on an Ender 3, M3 screws into brass heat-set inserts

Clamp-on only

Two hose clamps on the barrel, a saddle clamp on the trigger guard

23 days

From measuring the gun to the first clay broken by the machine

This is the gun as it sits today, right side. Up front: camera in the nose, a Raspberry Pi, a 3-cell battery and two voltage regulators, held by two worm-drive hose clamps seated in grooves. At the guard: a hobby servo beside the trigger with a printed cam reaching inside the guard, and the arming button. Hard requirement from day one: no drilling, no permanent changes to the gun. 8 parts in the front module, 4 in the trigger set.
