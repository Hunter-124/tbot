Servo beside the guard, cam on the trigger’s lower hook

Command to ignition: 161 ms → 62–70 ms with rev AH.

The trigger set went through the most change. The version I took to the first range day needed 161 milliseconds from command to the gun going off, and its printed servo mount broke after two shots. The mount was then redesigned twice: through-bolted with trapped nuts, then an anti-slide lock on the guard. Rev AH puts the servo body beside the guard with its output shaft inside it, and a printed cam presses the trigger’s lower hook. On the second range day the delay measured 62 to 70 ms, from recoil. Design revisions run G through AH, each logged with its reason.
